## English (en-US)
**Short description** (≤80):
```
Daily Torah study: Chok LeYisrael, Tehillim, Zohar, siddur, Hebrew calendar, zmanim
```
**Full description**:
```
Tamid is the daily learning app of the Bet El community: everything you need to keep up daily Torah study, in one place.

Chok LeYisrael
The traditional daily cycle - Torah with Rashi and Or HaChaim, Prophets and Writings, Tehillim, Mishnah, Gemara, Zohar, halacha and mussar - paced by today's date.

Ben Ish Chai
Daily halacha by the weekly parasha, in two yearly tracks, with a plain-language explanation and progress tracking.

Daily Tehillim
Monthly or weekly division, chapter tracking and gentle reminders.

Tikkunei HaZohar and Shnayim Mikra
Zohar in its season, and Shnayim Mikra before every Shabbat.

Mussar
Six classic mussar works, three units a day, with the option to browse the whole book.

Siddur, blessings and prayers
Sephardic (Edot HaMizrach) siddur, Birkat HaMazon, Meein Shalosh, blessings before enjoyment, Tefillat HaDerech and more, with search across all books. Every prayer has a compass towards Jerusalem, and Shacharit also has a tefillin mirror.

Hebrew calendar and daily times
Hebrew dates in letters with the Gregorian date underneath, candle lighting, Shabbat end, and daily times for your location. The weekly parasha opens in Shnayim Mikra.

Weekly booklet
A new study booklet every week, right on the home screen.

In memory of the tzaddikim
In the calendar, for every Hebrew date: the tzaddikim whose yahrzeit falls on that day.

Also: verse by name, dedication of your reading, yahrzeit reminders, daily reminders, QR share, night mode, reading settings (nikud, font, line spacing) and five languages - Hebrew, English, French, Russian and Georgian.

Free, no ads.
```
