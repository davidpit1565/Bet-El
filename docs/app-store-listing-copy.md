# App Store / Google Play listing copy

Ready-to-paste text for both stores. Hebrew is the primary listing language
(matches the app's default UI language); an English version is included
since both stores let you add a second localization and the app itself
ships English too.

## App name

**תָּמִיד · לימוד יומי**

(ASCII fallback if a store field rejects the Hebrew title outright:
**Tamid - Daily Torah Study**)

## Subtitle / short description

Apple "Subtitle" field (max 30 characters) and Google Play "Short
description" (max 80 characters) are different limits - two versions:

- Apple subtitle (≤30): `חוֹק לְיִשְׂרָאֵל וְלִמּוּד יוֹמִי`
- Play short description (≤80): `לימוד יומי מלא: חוק לישראל, תהילים, זוהר, בן איש חי ומוסר - הכל במקום אחד`

English equivalents:
- Apple subtitle: `Daily Torah Study, Made Simple`
- Play short description: `Daily Chok LeYisrael, Tehillim, Zohar, Ben Ish Chai and Musar - all in one app`

## Full description (English)

```
Tamid is a daily Torah study companion for the Beit-El community -
everything you need to keep a daily learning habit, in one app.

📖 Chok LeYisrael
The traditional daily study cycle - Torah with Rashi and Ohr HaChaim,
Nevi'im and Ketuvim, Tehillim, Mishnah, Gemara, Zohar, Halacha and Musar -
all on one shared daily pace, with progress tracking and reminders.

📕 Ben Ish Chai
Daily halacha study following the weekly Torah portion, across two study
tracks (Year 1 and Year 2), with plain-language explanations and
per-parasha progress tracking.

🕯️ Daily Tehillim
Monthly or weekly division, chapter-read tracking, and a gentle daily
reminder to finish today's portion.

✡️ Tikkunei HaZohar and Shnayim Mikra veEchad Targum
Zohar study in its season (Rosh Chodesh Elul through Yom Kippur) and
Shnayim Mikra before every Shabbat - with a Friday reminder.

🕊️ Musar for the Soul
Six classic Musar works at a shared pace of three units a day, with free
browsing anywhere in each book.

🗓️ Hebrew Calendar & Zmanim
Hebrew dates, candle-lighting/Havdalah times, and prayer times for your
location.

🔔 Daily Reminders
One fixed 08:00 reminder for each study track, so no day slips by.

🌐 Five Languages
Hebrew, English, French, Russian and Georgian.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no personal data collected - just a daily
study companion, for the merit of the many.
```
