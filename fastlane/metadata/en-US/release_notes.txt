What's new in 2.0.4:
• New compass widget pointing to Jerusalem: on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen
• Upgraded Live Activity: countdown to the next time of day with icons, a progress bar, and automatic roll-over between times
• Date converter in the calendar: Hebrew to Gregorian and back, with native iOS pickers
• Choosing a chapter, daf, aliyah or parasha in the books: a native menu in the header, and the header stays on every screen
• The Home screen always shows Chok LeYisrael and the Daily Zohar, by date
• Reading text on a soft rounded card, plus round iOS-style buttons and search
• Show/hide in the prayers: the whole card is tappable
• The Tefillin Mirror loads faster and works with a single tap
• The font size in Settings now updates, and commentary text follows the font size too
• Spelling fixes and stability improvements
