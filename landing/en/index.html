Good to know about us

No ads. No sign-up. No surprises.

Your progress stays with you

Your progress and settings are stored on your device only, and the app never asks for a name, email or phone number. We keep only an anonymous visit counter and “online now” count, anonymous usage statistics (hour, platform and rough region), and whatever you choose to send yourself: feedback, or a token for notifications if you turn them on. Details are in the privacy policy.

Transliteration, not translation

In Torah, Tehillim and prayers we copy the sound, not the meaning. No text is ever written or “corrected” without checking.

Not a halachic ruling

The app is a tool for learning and prayer. For halachic questions, ask your rabbi.

Thanks to our sources

Some of the texts and calendars are based on Sefaria. Thank you to everyone who made them accessible.

FAQ

Good to know.

What is in the app?

A full library for daily learning, organised by the day:

  • Chok LeYisrael: the daily learning order, with the weekly parasha, Tehillim, halacha and musar.
  • Daily Rambam, one or three chapters a day.
  • Daily Zohar and Tikkunei HaZohar, Tanya, Ben Ish Chai and the Shulchan Aruch HaRav.
  • Shnayim Mikra, Torah, Prophets and Writings, Mishnah and Gemara.
  • Musar: six musar books, three sections a day.
  • Siddur, blessings and prayers, from morning to night.
  • Hebrew calendar, daily times, tzaddikim yahrzeits and a weekly booklet.
  • Plus: a compass to Jerusalem and a tefillin mirror.
Which languages does the app support?

Hebrew, English, French, Russian and Georgian. In Torah, Tehillim, prayers and Zohar we write the sound in the letters of your language (transliteration) rather than translating the meaning, so you can read aloud properly.

Do I need internet to learn?

The app downloads its content in the background, and once it finishes you can learn without a connection.

Does the tefillin mirror record me?

No. The camera only shows a live picture and marks where the bayit should sit. No photo or video is saved or sent.

How does the compass know where to point?

It calculates the direction from your location to Jerusalem on the device itself, so it needs no internet connection, only that your phone can find your location. It also works in the browser version, on phones with a direction sensor. As with any phone compass, calibrate it from time to time.

Can I use it in a browser, without installing?

Yes. There is a browser version of the app, with the same learning and books. Open it from the link at the top of the page.

Is there an Android version?

The app is available on the App Store, and the Google Play version is on its way.

Is this a halachic ruling?

No. It is a tool for learning and prayer. For halachic questions, ask your rabbi.
